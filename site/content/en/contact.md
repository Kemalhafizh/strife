## What happens after you get in touch

1. **A 30-minute conversation.** We learn your architecture: databases, replication, and the risks that worry you most.
2. **A demo with your scenario.** We show Strife attacking a similar system, with the report and how to replay what it finds.
3. **A test plan for staging.** If it fits, we draft a test plan for your staging environment; your data stays on your network.

## Design partner program

We run a small program for banks and fintechs that want to run Strife on staging with us and take their first dossier into a real audit. Partners get hands-on support and help shape the product.
